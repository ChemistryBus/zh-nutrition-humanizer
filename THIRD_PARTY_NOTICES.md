# Third-party notices

This repository contains original code, documentation and rule tables, plus **short quoted
excerpts** from published Chinese nutrition references. The MIT licence in `LICENSE` covers the
code and the original writing only. It does **not** cover the excerpts below.

Each excerpt is 200–400 Chinese characters, kept for corpus statistics and side-by-side
comparison. Every file records `source`, `publisher`, `year`, `file`, `pages`, `slice`, `kind`
and `chars` in its front matter. No source PDF is redistributed, and no document is reproduced
in full.

**Rationale for the excerpts.** Register statistics require continuous prose in the target
style; a paraphrase would destroy exactly the features being measured (sentence length,
connective density, numeric formatting, term choice). The excerpts are therefore quotations
under fair-use-style limits: short, attributed, non-substitutive, and not usable to reconstruct
the work.

The 国家卫生健康委员会 食养指南 series is published as government documents for public
dissemination; excerpts are attributed with document title, publisher and page.

## Model-generated text (`eval/neg/`, `eval/neg_holdout/`, `examples/before.md`)

The negative samples and the "before" texts in the examples were generated by large language
models for this project. They contain no third-party text. Their prompts are recorded in
`CHANGELOG.md` (v0.3).

## Rewrites (`examples/after.md`, `eval/blind/packet*.md`)

The "after" texts are original rewrites of the model-generated drafts, produced for this repo.
They preserve every number, unit and evidence marker of the source drafts; no citation was
added.

## Tooling referenced but not bundled

- `liteparse` (for text-layer PDFs), `ocrmypdf` + `mineru` (for scanned PDFs) were used to build
  the corpus. None of them is redistributed here; the corpus extraction steps are documented in
  `references/baseline.md` §2.
- No model weights are included.

## Removal requests

If a rights holder considers an excerpt to exceed reasonable quotation, open an issue or contact
the maintainer; the excerpt will be removed and the affected statistics recomputed.
